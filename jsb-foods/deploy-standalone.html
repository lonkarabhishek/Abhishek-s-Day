The origin

From Mithila
to your munchies.

Bihar grows 90% of the world's makhana. We grew up on it — in steel tiffins, on temple prasad plates, tossed with ghee by our nanis.

JSB Foods began because the world was ready for the snack our grandmothers had been quietly perfecting for centuries.
